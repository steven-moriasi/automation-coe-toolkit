# Governance Checklist

## Intake and value

- [ ] Accountable sponsor and process owner are named.
- [ ] Current process, variants, exceptions, and pain points are documented.
- [ ] Volume, time, quality, and SLA baselines are measured.
- [ ] Desired outcome and non-goals are explicit.
- [ ] ROI assumptions have owners and confidence levels.
- [ ] Alternative simplification or elimination was considered before automation.

## Risk and architecture

- [ ] Data classification and retention are approved.
- [ ] Security, privacy, legal, and compliance triggers are reviewed.
- [ ] Human judgment and approval boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Identity, privilege, credential rotation, and audit design are approved.
- [ ] Failure, idempotency, retry, recovery, reconciliation, and rollback are documented.
- [ ] Dependency ownership, quotas, contracts, and exit strategy are known.

## Delivery and release

- [ ] Code, configuration, infrastructure, and migrations are versioned.
- [ ] Unit, contract, integration, security, and recovery checks pass.
- [ ] Artifact provenance and dependency audit are available.
- [ ] Runbook, SLOs, alerts, dashboards, and support ownership are ready.
- [ ] Change, release, rollback, and communications approvals are recorded.

## Operate and improve

- [ ] Outcomes are reconciled against source systems.
- [ ] Manual interventions and exceptions are measured.
- [ ] Incidents and control exceptions have owners and due dates.
- [ ] Realized outcomes are compared with the approved business case.
- [ ] Technical debt, model drift, vendor changes, and retirement are reviewed.
